A business networking group done differently.

We exist to build a trusted circle of independent professionals who refer each other with confidence. We do this through intentional relationships, honest support, and a shared commitment to helping each other grow and prosper.

What "differently" means

Referrals are a by-product of trust, not a quota.

Plenty of groups will hand you a lead sheet. We would rather you knew the person well enough to put your own name behind theirs.

Referrals you can stand behind

When a member sends you a name, they have already vouched for you. That only works if we know each other's work properly.

Relationships before transactions

Intentional relationships are the whole method. The business follows from them, rather than the other way round.

Honest support

Running something on your own is hard. This is a room where you can say so, and get a straight answer rather than a pitch.

Grow and prosper — together

Every member who gets stronger makes the circle worth more to everyone else in it. That is the network effect, literally.

How it works

Four steps, no mystery.

Come as a guest

Tell us you are coming, then sit in on a Wednesday meeting and see whether the room suits you.

Meet the members

Get to know what people actually do, and let them get to know what you do. Arrive 15 minutes early — that is when most of it happens.

Apply

Send in the form below. We will talk it through with you and let you know where things stand.

Start contributing

Show up, learn the other members' businesses, and pass on the referrals you would be happy to make yourself.

Questions

Before you apply.

When and where do you meet?

Wednesdays from 12 to 1:30pm at Sparkle Professional Cleaning, 5580 Power Inn Rd, Sacramento. Most people arrive about 15 minutes early to catch up before the meeting starts.

Can I visit before applying?

Yes — visiting first is the best way to work out whether the group is right for you. Let us know in the Discord that you are coming, or mention it in your application.

What does it cost to join?

Get in touch and we will walk you through the current membership terms before you commit to anything.

Do you take more than one person per profession?

Ask us when you apply. We will tell you honestly whether your line of work is already well covered in the room.

What is expected of members?

Turn up regularly, learn what the other members actually do, and pass on referrals you would be comfortable making with your own name attached. That is the whole deal.

Is the Discord open to non-members?

The invite is on this page, so come and introduce yourself — it is the easiest way to reach us between meetings.
