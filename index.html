A business networking group done differently.

We exist to build a trusted circle of independent professionals who refer each other with confidence, because we have taken the time to actually know each other's work, not just each other's business cards. We do this through intentional relationships, honest support, and a shared commitment to helping each other grow and prosper.

How we run it

A standing commitment, not a set of penalties.

On showing up

We ask members to treat Wednesday as a standing weekly commitment. Showing up consistently is part of what makes the trust and the referrals here real.

We have also deliberately not imported BNI's stricter attendance rules. There is no substitute-representative requirement, and no cap on how many meetings you can miss in a year. We trust each other to say when we cannot make it, and to show up as consistently as our businesses allow.

Positive reinforcement over policing attendance.

On the meeting itself

There is no fixed weekly format. The Convener draws from a standing menu of activities each week, choosing what fits the group's time, energy, and the news of the moment.

That keeps meetings responsive instead of rigid, the same instinct behind how we handle attendance and referrals.

Responsive, not rigid.

DEIB+ · On belonging

A bridge, not a mirror.

We want this to be a room where people show up as themselves, regardless of race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, religion, age, ability, or background, and are judged on the quality of their work and character, nothing else.

This group is meant to be a bridge, not a mirror: a place that connects people across difference, rather than one that just reflects who is already in the room.

Trust and referrals are easiest to extend to people who already look and sound like us. So as we grow, we will stay intentional about who is in the room, who is doing the inviting, and whether our own habits are quietly narrowing the circle instead of widening it.

Questions

Before you come along.

When and where do you meet?

Every Wednesday from 11:45am to 1:30pm at Sparkle Professional Cleaning, 5580 Power Inn Rd, Sacramento.

Can I visit before joining?

Yes. Visiting first is the best way to work out whether the group is right for you. Register on Eventbrite for the Wednesday you want to come along to, and we will be expecting you.

What does it cost?

Whatever a seat at the meeting costs is shown on the Eventbrite listing. Ask us about ongoing membership terms when you visit.

Do you take more than one person per profession?

Ask us when you visit. We will tell you honestly whether your line of work is already well covered in the room.

What is expected of members?

Treat Wednesday as a standing weekly commitment, learn what the other members actually do, and pass on referrals you would be comfortable making with your own name attached. That is the whole deal.

What happens if I miss a meeting?

You tell us, and that is it. We have deliberately not adopted BNI's stricter attendance rules: there is no substitute-representative requirement and no cap on how many meetings you can miss in a year. We would rather trust each other to show up as consistently as our businesses allow than police a register.

What actually happens in a meeting?

There is no fixed weekly format. The Convener picks from a standing menu of activities each week, based on the group's time, energy, and whatever is going on that week. It keeps the meeting responsive rather than rigid.

Is the Discord open to non-members?

The invite is on this page, so come and introduce yourself. It is the easiest way to reach us between meetings.
